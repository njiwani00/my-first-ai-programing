# Greenfield College Student Handbook (2026 Edition)

Greenfield College is a fictional college created for this RAG lesson. No AI model was trained on this handbook, so the only way a chatbot can answer questions about it is by retrieving the text below. That is exactly the problem RAG solves.

## Library

The Greenfield library is open from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM on weekdays and from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM on Saturdays. The library is closed on Sundays and public holidays. During the two weeks before final exams, the library stays open until midnight every day, including Sundays.

Students can borrow up to 5 books at a time for 14 days. A borrowed book can be renewed twice online if no other student has reserved it. The fine for a late book is 10 rupees per day, up to a maximum of 500 rupees per book. Lost books must be paid for at the replacement cost plus a 200 rupee processing fee.

Group study rooms on the second floor can be booked for up to 2 hours per day through the student portal.

## Attendance

Students must attend at least 75 percent of classes in every course to be allowed to sit the final exam. Medical leave with a doctor's certificate submitted within 7 days is not counted as an absence. Students who fall below 75 percent attendance receive a warning email and must meet their faculty advisor.

## Assignments and Late Submission Policy

Assignments are submitted through the GreenLearn portal before 11:59 PM on the due date. Late submissions lose 10 percent of the marks for each day they are late. Work submitted more than 3 days late receives zero marks. Extensions are granted only for medical or family emergencies and must be requested from the course instructor before the deadline.

## Plagiarism and AI Tools

Copying work from other students or from the internet without citation is plagiarism. A first offence results in zero marks for the assignment. A second offence is reported to the Academic Integrity Committee and can lead to suspension.

AI tools such as chatbots may be used for brainstorming and for checking grammar. Students must clearly state in their submission how they used any AI tool. Submitting AI-generated text as your own original work is treated as plagiarism.

## Exams and Grading

Final exams make up 60 percent of the course grade and internal assessments make up 40 percent. The passing mark is 40 percent overall. Students who fail a course may take a supplementary exam in July. Results are published on the student portal within 30 days of the last exam.

Grades are given as follows: O for 90 and above, A+ for 80 to 89, A for 70 to 79, B+ for 60 to 69, B for 50 to 59, C for 40 to 49, and F below 40.

## Hostel

Hostel gates close at 10:30 PM. Students returning later must inform the warden in advance. Visitors are allowed in the common room between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM. Cooking appliances are not permitted inside hostel rooms for safety reasons.

## Canteen and Wi-Fi

The canteen serves breakfast from 7:30 AM to 9:30 AM, lunch from 12:30 PM to 2:30 PM and snacks until 6:00 PM. Campus Wi-Fi is called GreenNet. Students log in with their student ID and portal password. Each student gets 5 GB of data per day.

## Scholarships

Students with a grade point average of 9.0 or above receive a merit scholarship covering 50 percent of tuition fees. Need-based scholarships are available for families with an annual income below 3 lakh rupees. Applications for both scholarships close on 31 August each year.

## Contact

For academic questions contact the Dean's office at dean@greenfield.example. For hostel issues contact the chief warden. For technical problems with GreenLearn or GreenNet, raise a ticket at the IT help desk in Block C.
